My daily bodyweight routine, done outside whenever possible. No gym needed.

## Philosophy

High frequency, moderate volume, full control. Each movement is performed with full range and deliberate tempo — no momentum, no ego.

## The Routine

### Push (daily)

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Push-ups (archer or ring) | 4 | 8–12 |
| Dips (parallel bars or bench) | 3 | 10–15 |
| Pike push-ups | 3 | 8–10 |

### Pull (daily)

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Pull-ups (pronated) | 4 | 6–10 |
| Chin-ups (supinated) | 3 | 8–12 |
| Inverted rows | 3 | 12–15 |

### Core (daily)

| Exercise | Sets | Duration |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Hollow body hold | 3 | 30–45 s |
| L-sit (parallettes) | 3 | 10–20 s |
| Hanging leg raise | 3 | 10–12 |

## Outdoor Parks

Find bars, rings, and parallel bars in public parks. Outdoor training improves focus and exposure to natural light.

{: .box-note}
**Note:** Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Rest a full day only when your body asks for it.
